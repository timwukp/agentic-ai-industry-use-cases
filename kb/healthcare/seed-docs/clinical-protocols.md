# Clinical Protocols Summary (Demo Health System)

Document ID: PRO-CLIN-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Chief Medical Officer

## Chronic disease management targets

- Type 2 diabetes: HbA1c target < 7.0% for most adults; individualize (< 8.0%) for
  limited life expectancy or hypoglycemia risk. A1C every 3 months until stable, then
  every 6 months. Annual eye exam, foot exam every visit, annual eGFR/UACR.
- Hypertension: target < 130/80 mmHg for high-risk patients; < 140/90 mmHg minimum
  standard. Recheck within 1 month of any medication change.
- Hyperlipidemia: moderate-to-high intensity statin for ASCVD 10-year risk >= 7.5%;
  lipid panel 4-12 weeks after statin initiation, then annually.

## Triage escalation

- EMERGENCY (RED): chest pain, dyspnea, altered mental status, stroke symptoms,
  anaphylaxis, seizure — direct to ED / 911. Document time of escalation.
- URGENT (ORANGE): high fever, severe abdominal pain, head injury, suicidal ideation —
  evaluation within 2 hours.
- Patients >= 65 or <= 5 years old are auto-escalated one triage level.

## Medication safety

- Verify allergies before any new prescription; penicillin-allergic patients require
  documented alternative selection.
- Interaction check required for every new prescription when the patient takes 2+
  medications. Major interactions require prescriber acknowledgment before dispensing.
- Hold metformin 48 hours before and after iodinated contrast; recheck renal function
  before resuming.
- High-alert medications (anticoagulants, insulin, opioids) require independent double
  check on dose changes.

## Readmission prevention

- All discharges with LACE+ risk score >= 30%: transitional care visit within 7 days,
  pharmacist medication reconciliation, and 48-hour follow-up call.
- Heart failure discharges: daily weight log education and 7-day cardiology follow-up.
