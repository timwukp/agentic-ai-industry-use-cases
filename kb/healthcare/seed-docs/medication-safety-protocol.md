# Medication Safety Protocol (Demo Health System)

Document ID: PRO-MEDSAFE-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Pharmacy & Therapeutics Committee

## High-alert medications

Anticoagulants (warfarin, DOACs), insulin, opioids, chemotherapy, and concentrated
electrolytes require:
- Independent double check on initiation and every dose change.
- Indication documented on the order.
- Patient counseling documented at first fill.

## Known major interactions (never dispense without prescriber acknowledgment)

| Pair | Risk | Standard action |
|------|------|-----------------|
| Warfarin + Aspirin | Bleeding, elevated INR | Avoid; if required, close INR monitoring |
| Sertraline + Tramadol | Serotonin syndrome | Avoid combination |
| Omeprazole + Clopidogrel | Reduced antiplatelet effect | Switch to pantoprazole |
| Atorvastatin + Amiodarone | Rhabdomyolysis | Cap atorvastatin at 40 mg/day |
| Metformin + iodinated contrast | Lactic acidosis | Hold metformin 48h pre/post |

## Polypharmacy review

- Patients on 6+ chronic medications: annual deprescribing review by clinical pharmacist.
- Fall-risk patients (Morse >= 45): review fall-risk-increasing drugs (sedatives,
  antihypertensives, hypoglycemics).

## Refill and renewal

- Zero-refill alerts route to the prescribing provider within 1 business day.
- Controlled substances: no automatic renewal; visit or telehealth review required
  per state regulation.

## Adverse event reporting

- Report suspected adverse drug events in the safety system within 24 hours.
- Severity grading follows NCC MERP categories; category E and above trigger root
  cause review.
