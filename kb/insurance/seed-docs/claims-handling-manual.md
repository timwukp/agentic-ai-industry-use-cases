# Claims Handling Manual (Demo Carrier)

Document ID: MAN-CLAIMS-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Claims Operations

## Intake standards

- Acknowledge every new claim within 24 hours; assign an adjuster within 48 hours.
- Required at intake: policy number, claim type, incident date, loss description,
  contact details. Verify the policy is active and premium paid to date before any
  assessment work.
- Late-reported incidents (> 30 days) are accepted but flagged for enhanced review.

## Assessment and documentation

- Minimum evidence: proof of loss, photos (auto/home/property) or medical records
  (health), police report (auto/liability), signed claim form.
- Severe or total-loss assessments require independent adjuster verification before
  any settlement offer.
- Every coverage determination must cite the applicable policy section and any
  exclusion considered.

## Settlement authority limits

| Role | Authority |
|------|-----------|
| Adjuster | Up to $10,000 |
| Senior adjuster | Up to $25,000 |
| Claims supervisor | Up to $100,000 |
| Claims director + committee | Above $100,000 |

- Settlements above $25,000 always require supervisor approval regardless of adjuster
  seniority.
- Depreciation and deductible must be itemized on every settlement calculation.

## Fair claims practices (state model act summary)

- Respond to claimant communications within 10 business days.
- Accept or deny a claim within 40 days of proof of loss, or send a written delay
  notice with reasons every 30 days thereafter.
- Deny claims only in writing, citing specific policy language.
- No settlement offer may be conditioned on releasing unrelated claims.

## Reserving

- Set an initial reserve within 5 business days of intake using severity class
  (low/medium/high/catastrophic) and the Chain-Ladder / Bornhuetter-Ferguson method.
- Review reserves at each stage change and at 90-day intervals.
