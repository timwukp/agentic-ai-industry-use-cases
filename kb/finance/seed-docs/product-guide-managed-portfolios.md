# Product Guide: Managed Portfolio Programs (Demo Firm)

Document ID: PRD-MP-003 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Product

## Core Portfolios (model-based)

- **Core Conservative** — 30% global equity / 65% investment-grade bonds / 5% cash.
  Fee 0.45%/yr. Rebalanced quarterly, ±3% drift bands.
- **Core Balanced** — 60/35/5. Fee 0.55%/yr. Benchmark: 60% MSCI ACWI / 40% Agg.
- **Core Growth** — 85/10/5. Fee 0.65%/yr. Suitable for Growth and Aggressive profiles only.

## Direct Indexing (SMA)

- Minimum $100,000. Tracks S&P 500 or Russell 1000 with per-client tax-loss harvesting.
- Fee 0.35%/yr. Tracking error target under 0.75%.
- Restrictions: client exclusion list up to 30 tickers; sector tilts within ±5%.

## Tax rules of thumb (US)

- Harvest losses only when replacement maintains exposure without violating the 30-day
  wash-sale window.
- Prefer selling lots held over 365 days when realizing gains (long-term rate).
- Municipal bond funds are generally unsuitable in tax-advantaged accounts.

## Rebalancing policy

Drift-band rebalancing: trade only asset classes outside their band; trade to band edge,
not to target, to reduce turnover. Estimated annual turnover: Core series 8–15%,
Direct Indexing 20–35% (harvesting included).
