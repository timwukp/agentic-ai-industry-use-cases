# Suitability Standards & Restricted Securities List (Demo Firm)

Document ID: POL-SUIT-002 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Compliance

## Client risk profiles

| Profile | Max equity allocation | Options | Leveraged ETFs | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 40% | No | No | No |
| Moderate | 70% | Covered calls only | No | Yes, ≤ 20% of equity |
| Growth | 90% | Yes (levels 1–2) | Yes, ≤ 10% of portfolio | Yes |
| Aggressive | 100% | Yes (levels 1–3) | Yes, ≤ 25% of portfolio | Yes |

A recommendation that moves a client outside their profile limits requires documented
supervisor approval before execution.

## Restricted list (no solicitation, no discretionary purchase)

- Securities of companies where the firm holds material non-public information.
- Pre-IPO and newly listed securities for the first 10 trading days.
- Any security under active SEC trading suspension.
- Demo restricted tickers for testing: ZZTOP Corp (ZZT), Example Industries (EXMP).

## Watch list (enhanced review, trades permitted)

- Securities with short interest above 20% of float.
- Securities down more than 30% in the trailing 5 sessions.

## Suitability documentation

Every solicited recommendation must record: client objective, time horizon, risk profile,
concentration after trade, and the rationale. The trading assistant must present a risk
warning with every recommendation and retain it in the conversation record.
