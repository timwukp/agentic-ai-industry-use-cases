# Margin Lending Policy (Demo Firm)

Document ID: POL-MARGIN-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Risk Committee

## Initial margin requirements

- Equities (listed, price ≥ $5): initial margin 50% (Reg T), maintenance margin 30%.
- Equities under $5, or with average daily volume under 100k shares: not marginable.
- ETFs (broad-market): initial 50%, maintenance 25%.
- Leveraged/inverse ETFs (2x/3x): initial 75%, maintenance 50%. 3x products require signed
  enhanced-risk disclosure.
- Options: no margin lending; long options must be paid in full. Covered calls permitted in
  margin accounts.

## Concentration surcharge

If a single security exceeds 40% of the account's gross market value, maintenance margin for
that position rises to 40% (equities) or 60% (leveraged ETFs).

## Margin calls

- Maintenance calls must be met within 3 business days (T+3) by deposit or liquidation.
- Calls over $250,000 escalate to the Risk Desk same day.
- The firm may liquidate without prior notice if account equity falls below 25% of gross
  market value.

## House rules stricter than Reg T

- Day-trading buying power capped at 3x maintenance excess (vs 4x regulatory).
- No margin on IPO securities for 30 calendar days after listing.
- Crypto-linked equities (exchanges, miners) carry maintenance margin of 40%.
