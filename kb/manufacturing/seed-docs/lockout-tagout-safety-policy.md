# Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Safety Policy (Demo Plant)

Document ID: POL-LOTO-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: EHS Department

## Scope

LOTO is mandatory for any servicing or maintenance where unexpected energization,
start-up, or release of stored energy could cause injury. This includes bearing
replacement, seal work, electrical testing, belt/coupling work, and any guard removal.

## The 7-step LOTO procedure

1. Prepare: identify all energy sources (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal,
   gravity, stored/spring).
2. Notify affected employees before shutdown.
3. Shut down the equipment using normal operating controls.
4. Isolate all energy sources (breakers, valves, blocks).
5. Apply personal locks and tags — one lock per authorized worker; group lockbox for
   crews.
6. Release/verify stored energy (bleed pressure, discharge capacitors, block elevated
   parts, allow cool-down).
7. Verify isolation: attempt start via normal controls, then return controls to OFF.

## Rules

- Only the worker who applied a lock may remove it. Supervisor removal requires the
  documented absent-worker procedure with EHS sign-off.
- Tags alone are not acceptable where a lockable isolation point exists.
- Shift handover with LOTO in place requires overlap: incoming worker applies their
  lock before the outgoing worker removes theirs.
- Test runs during maintenance require full LOTO removal, test, and re-application.

## Permits

- LOTO permit: required for all energy-isolating maintenance.
- Confined space entry permit: additionally required for vessel/pit/tank entry;
  atmosphere test before entry and continuous monitoring.
- Hot work permit: additionally required for welding/grinding; fire watch for 60
  minutes after completion.

## Enforcement and audit

- Annual LOTO audit per authorized employee; procedure review per equipment yearly.
- Violations: first = retraining; second = removal from authorized list.
- Work orders must list required permits before approval; maintenance may not start
  until the permits are issued.
