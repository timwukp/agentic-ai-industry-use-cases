# Appraisal Methodology Guide (Demo Brokerage)

Document ID: GUI-APPR-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Chief Valuation Officer

## The three approaches to value

1. Sales comparison approach (primary for residential): reconcile 3-6 recent,
   nearby, similar sales with itemized adjustments.
2. Income approach (primary for investment property): value = NOI / cap rate;
   verify rents against at least 3 rent comparables.
3. Cost approach (best for new or unique structures): replacement cost new minus
   physical, functional, and external depreciation, plus land value.

Use at least two approaches when data permits and reconcile with documented weighting.

## Comparable selection standards

- Sale date within 6 months preferred; 12 months maximum with time adjustment.
- Distance within 1 mile (urban/suburban) unless data is thin; document exceptions.
- Gross living area within ±25% of the subject.
- Adjust the COMPARABLE toward the subject (subject superior → positive adjustment).

## Standard adjustment ranges (this market)

| Factor | Adjustment |
|--------|------------|
| Gross living area | ~50% of market $/sqft per sqft difference |
| Bedroom | $5,000 - $15,000 |
| Bathroom | $8,000 - $20,000 |
| Market time | ~0.3% of price per month since sale |
| Condition step | $5,000 - $10,000 per grade |

Net adjustments above 15% or gross above 25% weaken a comparable; note it in the
reconciliation.

## USPAP conduct summary

- Be impartial and independent: no predetermined value, no fee contingent on value.
- Disclose all assumptions, limiting conditions, and data sources.
- An automated estimate (AVM/CMA) is NOT an appraisal; state this in every report.
- Retain workfiles for 5 years (or 2 years after final court disposition).

## Confidence reporting

- Always report a value RANGE with a confidence score, never a bare point estimate.
- Flag reliability risks: unusual property, thin comp data, rapidly moving market,
  or net adjustments beyond the standard ranges above.
