# Supplier SLA Standards (Demo Retailer)

Document ID: STD-SUP-001 | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Owner: Procurement Office

## Performance tiers

| Tier | Overall score | Consequences |
|------|---------------|--------------|
| PREFERRED | >= 90 | First allocation, multi-year contracts eligible |
| APPROVED | 75-89 | Standard terms |
| PROBATIONARY | < 75 | 90-day improvement plan; no new SKUs |

Overall score = 35% on-time delivery + 30% quality acceptance + 20% cost
competitiveness + 15% responsiveness.

## Minimum SLA requirements

- On-time delivery: >= 95% (measured against confirmed PO date, ±1 day window).
- Quality acceptance: >= 98.5%; defect rate <= 1,500 PPM.
- Lead time variability: <= 3 days standard deviation.
- Advance ship notice (ASN): 100% of shipments, sent >= 24h before arrival.
- Chargebacks apply for missed SLA: 2% of PO value per incident, capped at 8%.

## Purchase order rules

- All POs are Net 30 unless contract states otherwise.
- Supplier must confirm or reject a PO within 2 business days.
- No substitutions without buyer approval; unauthorized substitutions are rejected at
  the DC at supplier expense.

## Risk management

- Single-source SKUs: procurement must maintain a qualified alternate or an approved
  risk acceptance signed by the category manager.
- Suppliers in high-risk regions carry +15% safety stock on affected SKUs.
- Financial health review quarterly for suppliers with > $500K YTD spend.

## Escalation

1. Buyer-to-supplier scorecard review (monthly).
2. Probation with improvement plan (score < 75 or 2 consecutive missed SLAs).
3. Delisting decision by procurement council after failed improvement plan.
