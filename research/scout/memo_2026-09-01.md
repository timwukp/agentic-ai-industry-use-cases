### H1b candidates surveyed

| Candidate | Maturity / evidence | Deficiency-match | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explicit-duration HSMM** (replace incumbent's implicit geometric sojourn with an explicit non-geometric per-state duration model) | Formalism decades-mature and pedagogically taxonomized: "Explicit-Duration Markov Switching Models" (arXiv:1909.05800, 2019, a review monograph — maturity of the math, not itself new finance evidence). Financial-domain precedent exists but at a different task: a regularized VAR-HSMM was fit to NYSE portfolio data for regime *segmentation* with covariance shrinkage (arXiv:1804.10308, 2018) — that is a cross-sectional-p, not detection-lag, result; applicability caveat on this same line. | **Direct mechanism match**: `regime.py`'s own code confirms `persistence = 1/(1-p_stay)`, i.e. a literal geometric (memoryless) sojourn-time assumption — textbook root cause of "detects late because the model under-weights just-started persistence." | **Selected — see DRAFT protocol** |
| Nonlinear/kernel Granger causality variants (surveyed for H2, see below, cross-checked against H1b applicability) | N/A to H1b directly | N/A | not applicable |

**Counter-evidence actively sought for the HSMM candidate** (not just
supporting evidence):
- The duration hyperparameter (max support / order) has **no standard
  selection procedure** in the DDMS literature: "there is currently no
  procedure for estimating this duration or testing whether a given
  duration is appropriate for a given data set" (Reis & Pereira,
  arXiv:2307.01405, 2023) — this is exactly the failure class
  (data-driven hyperparameter silently defeats the mechanism) that
  collapsed Breitung-Candelon v1's frequency resolution
  (`findings_addendum.md`, BC section). Applied directly: the DRAFT
  protocol fixes `D_max=90` a priori rather than data-mining it.
- HSMM has strictly MORE parameters than the incumbent HMM (per-state
  duration distribution, not just a scalar self-transition probability),
  estimated from the SAME thin history of ≤18 complete regime sojourns per
  cohort — a small-sample estimation risk the skill's own precedent
  (BOCPD, signature features) shows is a live, not hypothetical, killer at
  PRISM's scale.
- The one closely analogous REAL trial on this exact deficiency
  (BOCPD v2, `findings_addendum.md`) found a large point-estimate lag gain
  (17.3bd mean, win-rate 0.684) that still **failed** significance at
  n=19 pooled episodes (p=0.172) — i.e., the base rate for "a structurally
  different detector beats the incumbent on lag, with enough certainty to
  clear a pre-registered gate" is, empirically, NOT high at PRISM's episode
  counts. This directly motivated the power check in §3 below.

### Power check (executed in code interpreter, this session)

Instrument as originally specified: sign-permutation (exact binomial) test
on episode WIN/LOSS only, pooling 3 primary cohorts (n=26 episodes).
Calibrated against the single real, documented analog effect size
available for "a structurally different detector challenges the incumbent
on H1b lag, on PRISM's own data": BOCPD v2's real trial, win-rate 13/19 =
0.684 (mean lag gain 17.3bd), which itself did NOT reach significance
(exact binomial p=0.172, n=19) — reported honestly in
`findings_addendum.md`.

Simulated power (20,000–4,000 seeds per cell, `scipy.stats.binomtest` /
`scipy.stats.wilcoxon`):

| n (episodes) | test | power at win-rate 0.684 (d≈0.479) |
|---|---|---|
| 19 (BOCPD's own precedent) | sign/binomial | ~0.15–0.23 |
| 26 (3-cohort pool, original design) | sign/binomial | ~0.30–0.41 |
| 59 (all 5 cohorts) | sign/binomial | ~0.80 (borderline, sign test only) |
| 19 | Wilcoxon signed-rank | ~0.48 |
| 26 | Wilcoxon signed-rank | ~0.64 |
| **59 (all 5 cohorts)** | **Wilcoxon signed-rank** | **0.944** |

**Verdict of the mandatory check**: the original 3-cohort/sign-test design
is **invalid** (<80% power at the one documented real effect size we
have) — this is disclosed as a redesign trigger, not hidden. **Redesign
applied before freezing** (permitted and required by the skill, since no
real-data result for THIS candidate exists yet to "shop" against):
Wilcoxon signed-rank (uses magnitude, not just sign) pooling all 5 cohorts
(n=59) clears 80% power (94.4%) at the documented effect size. This
redesign is now G2 in the DRAFT protocol. Caveat carried into the
protocol's §6: this calibrates against BOCPD's documented effect, not
HSMM's own (unknown) true effect — if HSMM's real effect is smaller, G2
may still be underpowered on real data, and that would read as a
legitimate null, not a design failure.
